    How to add an activity

    1. Tap Add activity at the bottom of the screen, or tap a “Nothing added” gap on the timeline.
    2. Choose what you did. Pick it from the list. Things you chose before are at the top.
    3. Say more, if asked. For example, how you travelled.
    4. Set the day and the time it started and ended. Times go in 15-minute steps. An activity can end the next day, like sleep.
    5. Tap Add to timeline. To add more in one go, tap Save and add another.

    Can't remember? Choose Forgot / Unspecified. To change or remove an activity, tap it on the timeline.

    When every day has activities, tap Submit at the top to check your diary and send it to the research team.

    Data protection

    The activities you add are saved on this phone straight away, even without an internet connection.

    They stay on this phone until you submit your diary. When the research team confirms they received it, you see a thank-you screen with a reference.

    After that, the diary is locked: you can look at it under Past diaries, but not change it.

    The research team's data protection statement goes here: who is responsible for the data, how it is stored and for how long, and your rights.
